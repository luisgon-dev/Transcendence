## Design Context

### Users
Transcendence serves both competitive climbers and casual players equally. Competitive players come for tier lists, matchups, and optimal builds — they need fast, trustworthy data. Casual players browse profiles, check post-game stats, and explore champions — they want it to feel welcoming, not overwhelming. The interface must serve dense analytics without alienating less hardcore users.

### Brand Personality
**Futuristic, immersive, edgy.** Transcendence leans into a sci-fi/cyberpunk-adjacent aesthetic — glowing accents, glass surfaces, aurora gradients, and deep dark backgrounds. It should feel like a high-tech command center for gaming data, not a sterile spreadsheet.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Dark-only, warm charcoal base (`20 14% 7%`) with amber gold (`38 78% 54%`) and warm coral (`16 62% 55%`) accents. Translucent layered surfaces with warm-tinted shadows. Colors are deliberately desaturated for refinement — never screaming.
- **Reference feel**: op.gg / u.gg information density — data tables, stat-heavy layouts, utilitarian structure — but wrapped in a warm, immersive dark aesthetic.
- **Anti-references**: Avoid looking like a generic SaaS dashboard (Linear, Notion). Avoid bubbly/playful/cartoon aesthetics. Avoid light mode. Avoid the cyan-on-dark / purple-gradient AI slop palette.
- **Typography**: Space Grotesk for headings (geometric, technical), Plus Jakarta Sans for body (clean, readable).
- **Motion**: Subtle shimmer and float animations. No heavy page transitions or distracting motion.
- **Brand mark**: Amber-to-coral gradient (`#f0a030` → `#e06545`), rounded square icon.

### Design Principles
1. **Data density over decoration** — Show the numbers. Tables, stats, and rankings are the product. Visual flair enhances readability, never competes with it.
2. **Glass, not clutter** — Use glassmorphic surfaces and subtle depth to organize information hierarchically. Every card and panel should feel like a window into data, not a container around it.
3. **Glow with purpose** — Amber/coral accents and glow effects highlight what matters: primary actions, tier rankings, win rates. Never glow decoratively.
4. **Inclusive by default** — Good contrast ratios, keyboard navigability, and readable type sizes. No strict WCAG compliance target, but accessibility should never be an afterthought.
5. **One mode, no compromises** — Dark only. Every color, shadow, and gradient is tuned for dark backgrounds. This focus enables deeper visual polish.
